1. Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how Shopora Market Store may use cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies when you visit shoporamarketstore.store.

Some technologies are necessary for website features to work correctly, while others may be used by third-party services that appear on or interact with the website.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites can store on a visitor's device through their web browser.

They can help websites remember information, maintain preferences, understand website usage, or provide certain functionality.

Cookies may be temporary and expire when a browser session ends, or they may remain on a device for a longer period depending on how they are configured.

3. Browser Local Storage

Shopora Market Store currently uses browser local storage for certain shopping features.

Local storage is different from traditional cookies. It allows a website to store information directly in the browser for use by that website.

Shopping Cart

When you add products to your cart, your browser may locally store product information and quantities so that your cart can remain available as you navigate between Shopora pages.

Wishlist

Products saved to your wishlist may also be stored locally in your browser so that your saved products remain available on the website.

4. How Browser Storage Supports the Website

Browser storage may be used to:

  • Remember products added to your shopping cart
  • Remember products saved to your wishlist
  • Maintain quantities selected for cart items
  • Improve navigation between shopping pages
  • Support basic website functionality

This locally stored information is primarily used to provide the shopping functionality visible to you on the website.

5. Third-Party Services

Shopora Market Store may use external services that have their own cookies, storage technologies, analytics, advertising technologies, or other tracking mechanisms.

Examples may include website hosting, analytics, advertising, fonts, communication services, or other third-party resources.

Third-party services operate under their own policies. Shopora Market Store does not control how independent third parties process information through their services.

External Websites

Links to other websites may be available through Shopora Market Store. Those websites may use their own cookies and similar technologies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of any external website you visit.

6. Your Cookie & Storage Controls

Most modern browsers provide settings that allow you to control cookies and website storage.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • Block or restrict cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Clear website data
  • Clear local storage
  • Configure permissions for individual websites

If you clear Shopora Market Store's local storage, information such as your saved cart and wishlist may be removed from your browser.

Please note: Blocking or clearing storage may affect some shopping features of the website.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, third-party services, technology, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
