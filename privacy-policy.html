Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit and use the Shopora Market Store website.

1. Overview

Shopora Market Store respects your privacy and aims to be transparent about how information is handled through this website.

This Privacy Policy applies to shoporamarketstore.store and explains the general practices used when visitors browse the website, interact with products, use shopping features, or contact Shopora Market Store.

By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the website, information may be provided directly by you or handled automatically by your browser.

Information You Provide

If you contact us or submit an order request through the website, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery or contact address
  • City, province, or postal information
  • Product and order details
  • Any message or notes you choose to send

Automatically Handled Information

Basic technical information may be made available through normal website operation, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, approximate usage information, and technical information required for the website to function.

3. Local Storage

Shopora Market Store uses browser local storage to provide shopping features such as your cart and wishlist.

Depending on your actions, your browser may locally store information such as selected products, product quantities, and wishlist items.

This information is stored in your browser and is used to maintain your shopping experience across pages on the website.

Clearing your browser's local storage or website data may remove locally saved cart and wishlist information.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and improving website functionality
  • Remembering shopping cart and wishlist selections
  • Responding to questions and customer messages
  • Processing and reviewing order requests
  • Communicating with you regarding a request you have submitted
  • Maintaining website security and functionality
  • Understanding general website usage and improving the user experience

Shopora Market Store does not need access to your browser's locally stored wishlist or cart simply for you to browse the website.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services or external resources for functionality such as fonts, website hosting, analytics, advertising, communication, or other services.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Shopora Market Store does not control the privacy practices of external websites or services.

External Links

Shopora Market Store may contain links to external websites. Visiting an external website means that its own privacy policy and terms may apply.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of external services before providing them with personal information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep website operations secure and to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, misuse, or loss of information.

However, no website, online service, or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Shopora Market Store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

8. Your Choices

You can control certain information stored locally in your browser through your browser settings.

You may also choose not to provide optional information when contacting us. However, some information may be necessary for us to respond to an inquiry or process an order request.

You can contact Shopora Market Store if you have a question about information you have provided to us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, legal requirements, or business practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
