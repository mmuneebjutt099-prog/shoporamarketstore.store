Shipping, Delivery and Returns: What to Check Before Ordering

A product is only part of an online order. Delivery estimates, shipping charges, address accuracy and return conditions can affect whether a purchase works for you. Review these details before placing an order so you know what to expect.

Shopora Editorial Team · 6 min read

1. Read the delivery estimate carefully

Check the delivery estimate shown for your destination and selected shipping option. An estimate is not necessarily a guaranteed date. Processing time, carrier transit, weekends, public holidays and local conditions can affect arrival. If timing is essential, confirm the current estimate before ordering.

2. Check the full delivery address

Review the recipient name, phone number, street or house details, city and postal code before submitting checkout. Use the format requested by the store. An incomplete or inaccurate address can delay delivery or make the parcel difficult to hand over.

3. Understand shipping charges and tracking

Check whether shipping is included in the displayed price or added at checkout. If tracking is offered, use the official tracking link or carrier website and keep the order reference. Tracking events may update at different stages, so consult the carrier's guidance if a status appears unclear.

4. Read the return and refund policy before buying

Look for the return window, item-condition requirements, exclusions, proof-of-purchase requirements and instructions for starting a return. Do not assume every product can be returned or that return shipping is always free. Follow the store's current written policy and applicable consumer rules.

5. Know what to do if a parcel arrives damaged

Keep the packaging and order details until you have checked the item. If there is damage, a missing part or the wrong product, document the issue with clear photos where appropriate and contact the store through its published support channel. Follow any stated time limits for reporting a problem.

6. Keep a record of the order

Save the confirmation, checkout total, delivery information and support messages. These records help you identify the exact order and explain the issue if you need assistance.

Quick checklist

  • Delivery estimate and shipping charge are clear.
  • The delivery address and contact number are correct.
  • I know how to access tracking if it is offered.
  • I have read return eligibility and reporting steps.
  • I will keep the confirmation until the order is resolved.
