How to Measure and Compare Home & Living Products Online

Measure your space and check materials, care, assembly, package contents and delivery details before choosing home products.

Shopora Editorial Team · 6 min read

SHOPORA FIELD GUIDEMEASURE FIRST

1. Measure the space and access route

Before choosing furniture, storage or decor, measure the location where it will be used. Check width, height, depth and clearance for doors, drawers or movement. For larger items, consider the route through doorways, hallways and stairs as well as the final placement.

2. Compare materials and finishes

The material and finish can affect appearance, cleaning, durability and everyday care. Read the specifications instead of assuming that an item is solid wood, metal, glass or another material because of how it looks in a photo. Check cleaning instructions and whether assembly or special maintenance is required.

3. Check intended use and care labels

For kitchen and household items, verify the stated intended use and care information. Do not assume an item is microwave-safe, dishwasher-safe, heat-resistant or suitable for food contact unless the listing or product labeling explicitly confirms it.

4. Confirm package contents

Lifestyle photographs may include props or coordinating items that are not part of the product. Check the listed quantity, dimensions, set contents, hardware and assembly parts. If the product is sold as a set, confirm the number and type of pieces included.

5. Plan for delivery and damage checks

Review the delivery information and any conditions for bulky or fragile products. Keep the order confirmation and inspect the item after delivery. If something arrives damaged or differs from the listing, follow the store's stated support process and retain relevant photos and packaging where appropriate.

Quick checklist

  • Measure the intended space and access route.
  • Confirm materials, dimensions and finish.
  • Review care and safe-use information.
  • Check quantity, assembly needs and package contents.
  • Review delivery and damage-reporting terms.
