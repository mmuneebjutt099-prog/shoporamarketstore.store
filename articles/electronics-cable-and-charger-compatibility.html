Chargers and Cables: Check Compatibility Before Buying | Shopora Market Store

A cable or charger can look right and still be unsuitable for a particular device. Check the exact connector, supported standards and power requirements before buying.

Shopora Editorial Team · 6 min read

1. Identify the exact device

Find the device model in its settings, on its label or in the manufacturer’s documentation. Compatibility can differ between products that look similar, so use the exact model rather than a broad brand name.

2. Match the connector at both ends

Check the connector required by your device and the connector that plugs into the power source or accessory. Similar-looking ports are not always interchangeable. Confirm whether you need USB-C, Lightning, Micro-USB or another connector.

3. Understand power ratings

Review the charger’s output voltage, current and wattage alongside the device’s requirements. A higher wattage label alone does not guarantee faster charging; supported charging protocols and the cable can affect performance. Follow the manufacturer’s specifications.

4. Check data and feature support

Some cables are designed mainly for charging, while others also support data transfer, video or particular accessories. Read the stated capabilities and do not assume every cable with the same connector supports the same features.

5. Prefer clear specifications and safe use

Look for readable ratings, manufacturer details and relevant safety information. Avoid damaged accessories and stop using a charger that becomes unusually hot, smells burnt or shows visible damage. Use reputable products that meet the device’s requirements.

Final compatibility checklist

Confirm device model, connector on both ends, power requirements, supported features and package contents. If the listing does not provide enough information, ask the seller or consult the device manufacturer before ordering.

Quick checklist

  • Product details and intended use are clear.
  • Dimensions, ingredients or compatibility details are checked where relevant.
  • Package contents and quantity are confirmed.
  • Delivery and return terms have been reviewed.
