Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Shopora Labs handles information when you visit shoporamarketstore.store and use the tools and guides published here.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, message, and any details needed to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Browser-based tools

The current search snippet preview and SEO checklist run in your browser. Text entered into the snippet preview is used to update the preview on the page. Checklist selections are used to update the progress display. These tools do not submit that content to our servers.

Server and security information

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information in server logs, such as requested page, date and time, browser type, and network information, to deliver and protect the website. Hosting providers may retain logs under their own policies.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently operate account login or payment features on this website. If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are added later, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices and choices before those services are used.

External links

Our pages may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

Your choices

You can choose not to email us. You can also clear browser data through your browser settings. For privacy questions, contact brandcompany815@gmail.com.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.
