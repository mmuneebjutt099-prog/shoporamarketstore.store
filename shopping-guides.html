A practical guide to shopping online with confidence

A product image can catch your eye, but the right purchase depends on details: fit, materials, compatibility, total cost and the rules that apply if something does not work out. Use this guide as a checklist when comparing products on Shopora or any online store.

Start with five checks for any product

  1. Identify the exact item. Read the full title and description. Similar-looking products can have different sizes, materials, functions or included components.
  2. Confirm the selected variant. Check the chosen colour, size, model, pack quantity or other option before adding the item to your cart.
  3. Look for useful specifications. Dimensions, materials, care instructions, compatibility details and what is included are more helpful than broad marketing phrases.
  4. Calculate the full cost. Review the current price and all delivery or other charges shown for your order before you pay.
  5. Read the policies. Understand delivery, cancellation, returns and refunds. If something important is unclear, ask the store before ordering.

How to compare clothing and fashion accessories

Clothing sizes are not consistent across every brand or design. A label such as small or medium is only a starting point; measurements are a more useful comparison when they are provided.

  • Compare the garment's chest, waist, hip, shoulder or length measurements with a similar item that fits you well.
  • Check fabric composition and care instructions if listed. Fabric content can affect stretch, warmth, drape and washing needs.
  • Use photos for visual reference, but read the description to confirm pockets, closures, included pieces and other specifications.
  • For bags, jewellery and other accessories, check dimensions, fastening type, material and the number of pieces included.
  • Review the return policy before buying if fit or colour is especially important to your decision.

Useful question: Do the listed measurements describe the item itself or the body measurements it is designed to fit? Ask support if the listing does not make this clear.

What to check when comparing beauty products

Beauty items can differ in shade, formula, volume and intended use even when the packaging looks similar. Check the exact product information rather than assuming two items are interchangeable.

  • Confirm the exact shade, size or volume and the number of units in the package.
  • Read the ingredient list, usage directions and warnings when available, especially if you need to avoid a particular ingredient.
  • Check storage instructions and any stated expiry or batch information supplied with the product.
  • Do not rely on a product name alone for medical, skin-treatment or allergy advice. For health concerns, consult a qualified professional.
  • Review the store's return terms; hygiene-sensitive products may have specific conditions.

How to check electronics and tech accessories

Before buying an electronic item, verify that its specifications match the device and setup you already own. A familiar product photo does not guarantee that the connector, voltage, capacity or model is right for you.

  • Check model compatibility, connector type, supported operating system or device, and listed technical requirements.
  • For chargers and powered devices, verify the stated input, output, plug type and applicable power requirements.
  • For storage, audio or connectivity products, check capacity, supported formats, wireless standards or range when relevant.
  • Confirm whether cables, adapters, batteries, manuals or other accessories are included or must be purchased separately.
  • Read warranty information if stated. Do not assume a warranty exists unless the product or seller terms confirm it.

Compatibility rule: Match the product's exact specification to your own device model. If the listing does not provide enough information, pause and ask before ordering.

Choosing home and living products

For household items, measurements and materials often matter more than a staged photograph. Check the available space and the product's intended use before deciding.

  • Measure the place where the item will be used, including width, height, depth and clearance needed for doors or drawers.
  • Check material, finish, cleaning instructions and whether assembly is required.
  • For kitchen or food-contact items, read stated care and safe-use information; do not assume an item is microwave-safe, dishwasher-safe or heat-resistant unless specified.
  • Confirm the quantity in a set and whether decorative objects shown in photos are included.
  • For fragile or bulky items, pay attention to delivery information and the process for reporting damage.

Before you place an order

  1. Reopen the product page and confirm the selected item and variant.
  2. Check the current price and the full order total displayed at checkout.
  3. Enter a complete delivery address and a contact number you can access.
  4. Read the return and refund policy and review the privacy policy for how personal information is handled.
  5. Use the available checkout payment options and keep your order confirmation for reference.
  6. If the product description, delivery terms or payment total appears inconsistent, contact support to clarify it before completing the order.

Frequently asked questions

What if a listing does not show a specification I need?

Contact Shopora before ordering and name the exact product and specification you need to confirm. Do not assume a detail that is not stated.

Can I rely on product photographs for colour or size?

Use photos as a visual reference, but read the written details and measurements. Display settings, lighting and photography can affect how colours appear on a screen.

Where can I check whether delivery is available?

Use the current checkout flow and its delivery information for your address. If an option or cost is unclear, contact support before completing the order.

Where do I find the return rules?

Read Shopora's return and refund policy before purchasing. If you need clarification about a particular item, contact support.
